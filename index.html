Our Heritage

Established in 2020, Alkari Group is a Jakarta-based holding and consulting platform focused on building enduring value across a growing portfolio of businesses and strategic ventures.

As businesses evolve, clarity can become difficult to maintain. Alkari Group works closely with companies to restore structure, strengthen direction, and create operational alignment that supports sustainable progress.

Guided by agility, stewardship, and strategic thinking, we believe growth should be intentional. Through our advisory capabilities and subsidiaries, Alkari Group continues to cultivate opportunities across industries.

Our Services

Strategic Consulting

Delivering elite corporate advisory, operational restructuring, and management consulting. We optimize enterprise architecture to restore operational clarity and foster institutional longevity.

Venture Cultivation

Identifying and nurturing high-potential market opportunities. We deploy disciplined capital and strategic stewardship to guide early and mid-stage ventures toward scalable success.
