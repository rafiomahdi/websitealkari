Our Heritage

Established in 2018, Alkara Indonesia is a Jakarta-based holding and consulting platform focused on building enduring value across a growing portfolio of businesses and strategic ventures.

As businesses evolve, clarity can become difficult to maintain. Alkara works closely with companies to restore structure, strengthen direction, and create operational alignment that supports sustainable progress.

Guided by agility, stewardship, and strategic thinking, we believe growth should be intentional. Through our advisory capabilities and subsidiaries, Alkari Group continues to cultivate opportunities across industries.
